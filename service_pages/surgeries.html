Surgeries & Orthopedic
Booking

Description

Spay/castration for dogs & cats, skin abscesses or lacerations, fracture repair, intestinal obstruction due to foreign body, mass or tumor removal, bladder stones or urethral blockages, and malignant skin tumors.

How It Works

Before surgery, your pet undergoes a medical assessment to determine the safest treatment plan. The procedure is then carried out by qualified veterinary professionals using appropriate surgical techniques and monitoring equipment. After surgery, pets receive post-operative care and recovery guidance to support healing.

Benefits

Advanced Medical Care

Provides treatment for injuries, illnesses, and serious medical conditions.

Improved Mobility and Comfort

Orthopedic procedures help restore movement and reduce pain.

Safe Surgical Procedures

Pets are carefully monitored throughout surgery and recovery.

Better Quality of Life

Successful treatment can greatly improve your pet’s health and wellbeing.
