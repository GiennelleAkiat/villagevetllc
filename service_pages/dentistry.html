Dentistry
Booking

Description

Your pet needs a healthy mouth to be happy and healthy! Our practice provides a complete line of veterinary dentistry services, including professional dental cleanings and teeth extractions. We work to help you avoid dental problems that might harm your pet's heart and organs!

How It Works

Pre-Dental Examination: We perform an examination of your pet's teeth and conduct a pre-anesthetic blood test to confirm that they are in good condition for the treatment. Deep Dental Cleaning: Your pet is placed under controlled anesthesia, where plaque and tartar are removed from their teeth above and below the gum line. Post-Care Tips: Your pet is allowed to wake up after a successful procedure, and we give you instructions on how to maintain their dental health at home.

Benefits

Fresher Breath

Say goodbye to the unpleasant "doggy"or "kitty" breath.

Pain Prevention

Prevents tooth decay and gum disease before they cause severe, hidden pain.

Better Overall Well-being

Getting rid of oral bacteria protects vital organs such as the heart and kidneys.

Better Appetite & Mood

Pets with healthy teeth can chew comfortably and enjoy their favourite chew toys again.
