Microchipping
Booking

Description

A microchip is the only permanent identification form that can reunite you with your beloved pet. Unlike collars and ID tags, which can break or become worn and illegible, a microchip will last for the life of your pet.

How It Works

A tiny microchip, about the size of a grain of rice, is gently inserted beneath your pet’s skin using a quick and safe procedure. Each chip contains a unique identification number linked to your contact information in a secure database, which can be scanned by veterinary clinics or shelters.

Benefits

Permanent Identification

Provides lifelong identification that cannot easily be removed or lost.

Quick Recovery Assistance

Improves the chances of reuniting lost pets with their owners.

Safe and Simple Procedure

The insertion process is fast, minimally invasive, and safe for pets.

Peace of Mind

Gives owners reassurance knowing their pet has reliable identification.
