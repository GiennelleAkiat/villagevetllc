Diagnostic Imaging
Booking

Description

There may be instances where even a physical examination is not sufficient. Our advanced diagnostic imaging capabilities provide a non-invasive and painless method of viewing the inner workings of your pet’s body. This helps us to detect hidden bone fractures, swallowed items, or other conditions.

How It Works

Painless Preparation: Your pet will be made comfortable during their procedure, with mild sedatives used only if absolutely necessary. The Imaging: Using our cutting-edge technology that emits low levels of radiation, we can produce high-quality pictures of bones, organs, or tissues. Professional Analysis: Your veterinarian can immediately view the results of our digital imaging in real-time to accurately diagnose what is happening inside your pet’s body.

Benefits

Completely Non-Invasive

It provides a non-surgical method of diagnosing internal health problems.

Instant Results

The results of a digital X-ray and ultrasound are immediately analyzed digitally.

Early Detection

It can help us detect cancerous growths, pregnancy, or joint problems at an early stage.
